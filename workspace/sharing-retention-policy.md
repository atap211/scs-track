## 1. Where files live
All active business records, customer lists, price sheets, and quotes live in the company Shared Drives so the business owns the files rather than individual staff accounts. Personal My Drive folders are used only for temporary working drafts before moving them to a Shared Drive.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is restricted to approved partner and supplier domains only, and public "Anyone with the link" sharing is turned off by default. Staff must share specific files as Viewer or Commenter rather than sharing entire folders externally.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff in the assigned Organizational Units may sign in, and 2-Step Verification is required for every account with backup codes kept offline on paper. Mobile devices accessing work email or Drive must have a screen lock enabled under Basic mobile management.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Invoices, tax records, signed quotes, and customer registers are kept in the Finance and Operations Shared Drives for 2 years (exact statutory period to be checked with the owner). Deleted Shared Drive files remain recoverable in Trash for 30 days before permanent removal.

## 5. The monthly export
On the first Monday of each month, the designated admin runs a Google Takeout export of key Shared Drive folders and stores the dated archive on an encrypted external backup drive kept off-site. One random file from each monthly export is opened and verified before the old archive is rotated.

## 6. Who holds admin
Only the business owner holds full Super Admin access using a dedicated recovery account, while day-to-day onboarding and password resets use a scoped User Management Admin role. Admin accounts never share passwords and keep paper backup codes locked in the office safe.

## 7. When someone leaves
When a staff member leaves, the admin exports their Drive data via Takeout, transfers ownership of their My Drive files to their manager, and suspends the account on the same day. Accounts are never deleted immediately so audit logs and shared file history remain intact for review.
